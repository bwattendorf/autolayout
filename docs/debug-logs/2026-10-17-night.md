## Experiments and results

### Finding 1 (2026-10-17, measured): the placer sweep was judged at the wrong rules

- First `tools/place_sweep.py` run (`out/night_place1`, rules = the freeplace `.kicad_pro`): the five small
  boards (g2c, g2i, g2io, g2n, g2o) came out at KiCad 0 unconnected, every large board left 56-99 open
  (c1 82, c2 82, c2g 73, c4 88, cm 75, e1 56, e2 70, el 81, g2 99, m1 63).
- Probe: `route --explain` on m1's placement: nearly every unreached pad is a pin of U1 (the fine-pitch IC),
  router pitch 0.505 mm. `tools/rules_of.py`: the freeplace `.kicad_pro` carries the generic defaults
  (track 0.25, clearance 0.2, via 0.6/0.3); the service's fab/returned boards carry 3.5 mil (track and
  clearance 0.0889, via 0.25/0.15).
- Test: `tools/route_with_pro.py out/halo_m1/m1_s1.kicad_pcb <fab m1.kicad_pro>`: **our default placement of
  m1, routed under the fab rules: router unconnected 0, nets 104/104** (it was 65 at the freeplace rules).
  So the placer is not the problem on m1; the sweep measured it at rules the board is not made with.
- Fix: `place_sweep.py` now routes and judges each placed board under the fab rules by default
  (`--rules fab`; fab/finish/returned-3p5mil project file; freeplace file only when none exists).
  Re-run: out/night_place2.
- Ruled out on the way (all at the wrong rules, so only the direction is informative): global
  `--halo-scale` 1/1.5/2/3 -> 65/69/60/60 open on m1; `--spread` 69 (scale 1), 54 (scale 2);
  per-part halo boost feedback (`tools/autoplace.py`) 65 -> 59; `--tap-spread` 65 -> 62.
  Density: our c1 layout clusters in the middle (34 of 100 cells empty, peaks of 9) vs the service's (19
  empty); the service's m1 layout is about as clustered as ours (39 vs 46 empty) and routes at 0.

### Finding 2 (2026-10-18, measured): two locked capacitors sat off the board

- With fab rules, `out/night_place2` (our placement + router, default): 17 of 19 boards at KiCad 0; c2 1 open
  (C29.1, +12V) and c4 2 open (C57). `route --explain` gave no reason; the new "unexplained" line said the
  pad has 0 landing cells; `tools/near_pads.py` found nothing within 3 mm; `tools/density.py` printed the
  outline: C29's pad at x = 48.59, the board starts at x = 50.00 (C57: y = 257.5, board ends at 253.2).
  Both are `(locked yes)` in the freeplace input; the service moved them.
- Fix: `group::locked_off_board` (a locked part with a pad, mounting holes aside, whose centre is outside the
  outline is released to the placer; geometry only; named in the place log), with a unit test.
  Result (`out/ps_c2c4`): c2 499 -> 0 (150/150 nets), c4 499 -> 0 (190/190), no new violations.
