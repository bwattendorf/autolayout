# Round 18: why our footprint counts exceed the service's (2026-10-14)

Base hash c9660df. New tool: `tools/footprint_diff.py` (reads files only, KiCad python). Command per board
(measured, exit 0 for all 8): `"$KPY" tools/footprint_diff.py NETLIST FREEPLACE RETURNED` with RETURNED the newest
`3p5mil-candidate*` used in Round 17 (c4: candidate3). Raw output: `2026-10-14-round18/<sku>.txt`.

## Headline
1. **Every returned board is older than its freeplace board** (returns 31 Aug 09:15-16:03; freeplaces 1 Sep 09:21-10:15). So the "dropped" parts are most likely not dropped by the service: the freeplace board was regenerated afterwards with parts the service was never sent. Not proven per part (we do not have the upload timestamp or the CSVs the service saw).
2. Every dropped reference is a diode or LED (status lamps, SS34, BAT54S, one SMBJ28CA). No connector, passive or mechanical part was dropped on any board.
3. Netlists (13 Oct) are newer still: each board has 2-6 resistors in the netlist that are in neither the freeplace nor the return (the `net_only` list: R54-R59 c1, R67-R72 c2, R68-R71 c2g, R89-R94 c4, R50-R53 e1, R75-R78 e2, R94-R97 m2, R28/R29 r). Those match the CLAUDE.md note for r/m2 and look like USB-C CC / reader resistors added after the freeplace was made; their values are 5.1k, 2.2k/5.6k/10k, 680R, 30k.
4. Reference numbers also shift: c1 dropped D17/D22/D23 but the return has an extra D19 (IOX_HB); c2 dropped D27 IOX_HB, D28 CLOCK, D29 BOOT, D21, D26 and has an extra D25 BOOT. The return's lamp references do not line up with the netlist's, so a reference-by-reference diff overstates "dropped" and understates "present under another name". Counts by value: c1 3 dropped vs 1 extra BOOT/HB-type lamp, c2 5 vs 1.

## Off-board caps (Step 6)
Both are +12V/GND 10 uF, F.Cu, **locked in the freeplace board itself** at the left edge (outline x starts at 50): c2 C29 at (49.5, 228.0), io C25 at (49.8, 205.5). Our placed board keeps them exactly there (locked parts are not moved), so their centres sit 0.2-0.5 mm outside the outline: that is the freeplace export, not our placer. The service has both on the board, unlocked: c2 C29 at (52.6, 228.3), io C25 at (52.6, 212.4) (shifted inward 3.1 mm, and 6.9 mm along the edge on io). The c2 J90 microSD flagged in Round 17 was not examined further.
