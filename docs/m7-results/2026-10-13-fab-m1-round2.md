## Where the verifier and KiCad disagree (the finding)

1. **Unconnected on Freerouting's board: KiCad 17, verifier 0.** The verifier does not see them.
   The 17 `unconnected_items` in `freerouting/m1_fab_routed_drc.rpt`:
   - 13 involve `Zone [GND]` on Top/Bottom Layer, all reported at (50.4250, 50.4250), i.e. GND zone fragments
     (zone-to-zone). The verifier is told the planes with `--plane` and evidently treats a plane as
     connected, not as a filled zone that can split into islands.
   - 1 is a pad pair: Pad 2 [GND] of C2 at (101.7206, 93.8032) to Pad 47 [GND] of U1 at (101.1000, 94.8500).
   - 3 are `+3V3` track pairs: (95.4582, 95.2794) to (95.2500, 95.5000); (102.6653, 97.9853) to (101.8218, 98.3000);
     (100.3000, 95.1956) to (101.3518, 93.3570).
   This is a real gap in the verifier, on a board it did not help make. On our board both say 0, so that
   agreement does not test whether it can see this kind of fault. I did not look at verifier source.
2. **Clearance on Freerouting's board: KiCad 2 clearance + 2 shorting_items, verifier 4 clearance.** Same
   four spots, the verifier labels the overlaps as clearance as the handoff predicted:
   - (98.9032, 86.5243) +1V1 vs N$54, 0.0722 mm (KiCad clearance) / verifier 0.0722 mm. Match.
   - (98.9032, 87.1595) +1V1 vs N$54, verifier -0.0704 mm (KiCad shorting_items, same items). Match.
   - (105.1313, 90.9107) N$85 vs +3V3_ADC, 0.0736 mm (KiCad clearance at 105.2183, 91.1377). Match, coordinates differ by item.
   - (105.4615, 91.2409) N$85 vs +3V3_ADC, verifier -0.0705 mm (KiCad shorting_items). Match.
3. **track_width 45 vs 45:** agree.
4. **via_dangling 9 on Freerouting's board:** KiCad only, as expected (not implemented). Coordinates of the first two:
   (115.1943, 113.3262) Via [N$67]; (103.9178, 93.2409) Via [+3V3].
